Welcome to Crossroads Fellowship

Crossroads Fellowship is a Southern Baptist church in Bixby, Oklahoma. We provide a casual atmosphere where people can worship, grow, fellowship and serve together.

Our worship style combines contemporary worship with the heritage of traditional hymns.
