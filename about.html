WHO WE ARE

A Church Family in Bixby

Crossroads Fellowship is a Southern Baptist church in Bixby, Oklahoma.

We strive to provide a casual atmosphere where people from all walks of life can come together to worship God, hear His Word, fellowship with other believers, and grow in their faith.

Our worship style combines contemporary worship with the heritage of traditional hymns.

OUR MISSION

Restoring Hurting People

To locate and restore broken and hurting people and release them as productive agents of Christ's mission in the world.

OUR PURPOSE

Membership, Maturity, Ministry & Mission

Our purpose is to magnify the Father as we seek to cross hurting hearts with the healing touch of God's hand.
